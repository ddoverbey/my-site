            No route imported.

            Map services & privacyCity and airport suggestions use Photon by Komoot; search text is sent as you type. Only driving and AWD endpoints are sent to the FOSSGIS routing service (OSRM). Imported GeoJSON/KML tracks are processed locally and are not sent to OSRM. Imported driving/AWD tracks clear land fog and award route counties. Each Ferry / Boat expedition reduces ocean fog by 10 percentage points, down to 50% opacity (99% before the first trip). Ferry and flight routes fully clear water within 100 miles (160.934 km) on either side of the logged route when the world atlas is loaded; unvisited land stays shrouded, and flight paths never clear land—only a small area around each flight's departure and arrival points. Ferry, flight, rail, and walking routes award no intermediate counties; rail and walking clear no route fog. Without an imported track, these routes are dashed direct approximations. Elevations use Open-Meteo. © OpenStreetMap contributors.
